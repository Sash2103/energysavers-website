Active Harmonic Filter

IEEE 519 · Harmonic Control in Electric Power Systems

An active harmonic filter (AHF) is a type of harmonic filter that uses active components, such as transistors and capacitors, to reduce harmonic current distortion on an electrical system. It can be used in industrial and commercial installations to eliminate harmonic current distortion and improve the efficiency of the system.

  1. Upper PCBA auxiliary air duct design

    Expansion of the heat dissipation channel for wide angle heat dissipation.

  2. IGBT PCBA precision components

    An AHF uses active components to detect and then neutralize harmonic current distortion.

  3. Inductors and IGBT heatsink: around 90% of the heat

    The IGBT heatsink and inductors, which account for most of the heat of the AHF, are concentrated in the lower layer with forced air cooling for fast heat dissipation, and do not affect the upper PCBA.

Our services

  1. Energy Audit

    An energy audit is an inspection and analysis of energy use and flows for energy conservation in a building, process or system to reduce the amount of energy input into the system without negatively affecting the output.

  2. PQ Audit and Measurement

    PQ Audit and Measurement is a process of measuring and evaluating the performance of an electrical system. It is used to identify potential problems, make recommendations for improvement, and monitor performance over time.

  3. Harmonic Studies

    Harmonic studies in power quality involve the analysis of the harmonic content of a power system. This involves the measurement of the voltage, current, and other power system parameters to determine the harmonic content, and to identify and quantify any harmonic distortion.

  4. Harmonic Simulation Studies

    Harmonic simulation studies in power quality involve the use of computer simulation to analyze the impact of harmonics on power systems.

  5. PQ Equipment Rental
